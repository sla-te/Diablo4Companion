# Diablo 4 itemization reference

Snapshot date: 2026-07-28. Patch described: 3.1.x, Season 14 "Death Awakening",
Lord of Hatred expansion. Patch 3.1.2 released on this same date (2026-07-28),
so a few items below reflect a mechanic that is only hours old at time of
writing. **Re-verify this entire file before relying on it in a later season or
patch** - D4 itemization changes every 3-4 months and several of the answers
here (enchanting Uniques/Mythics, the crafted-Mythic equip limit) were only
just changed this season.

Confidence tags: `[Certain]` = confirmed from an official Blizzard source
(patch notes / blue post) quoted directly. `[Likely]` = a single strong
secondary source (Maxroll, Icy Veins, game8), or two independent secondary
sources agreeing. `[Unverified]` = could not confirm, or sources conflict.

## 1. Item quality tiers and Ancestral

- Rarity tiers, low to high: Common, Magic, Rare, Legendary, Unique, Mythic
  (Unique). `[Likely]`
- **Ancestral is a quality tag layered on top of Legendary/Unique/Mythic
  items, not a separate rarity.** It starts dropping once a character reaches
  Torment difficulty. `[Likely]` (Icy Veins, Maxroll)
- Ancestral Legendary items drop at **900 Item Power** and are guaranteed at
  least one Greater Affix. `[Likely]`, sourced from a Season-14-dated game8
  page: "Torment Difficulties unlock Ancestral Legendary items, with 900 item
  power and a chance at Greater Affixes."
- Mythic Unique items (the rarest tier) drop at a fixed, higher Item Power
  than Ancestral Legendaries; sources disagree on the exact number (see
  Gaps).
- Sacred-quality items no longer drop; any older Sacred items a player still
  owns are tagged "Legacy." `[Likely]` (thegamer.com, Vessel of Hatred era -
  this specific fact is old but nothing found contradicts it for 3.1).

## 2. Item Power, "Quality NN (+NN)," and Masterworking rank

- **Item Power** sets a weapon's base damage, an armor piece's base defense,
  and the roll range of every affix on the item. `[Likely]` (thegamer.com,
  Maxroll)
- The tooltip's **"Quality"** value is exactly the item's **Masterworking
  rank**: every rank of Masterworking increases the item's Quality by 1, and
  Quality gives +1% to base Damage/Armor/Resistance and to every affix's
  value, up to +25% total at Quality/rank 25. `[Likely]` (Maxroll
  Masterworking Guide, "Updated for Season 11 - Rewrote the article for
  patch 2.5.0 Masterworking updates," cross-confirmed by the Blacksmith
  Upgrade & Salvage guide which is explicitly "Updated for Season 14").
- So "Quality 14 (+14)" on a tooltip means: this item has been Masterworked
  14 times, and is currently getting a cumulative +14% bonus to its base
  stats and every affix from that alone (Greater Affixes and the Capstone
  bonus stack on top separately).

## 3. Masterworking

- Available at the Blacksmith. Applies to Legendary, Unique, and Mythic
  Unique equipment (Rare items are not normally worth Masterworking).
  `[Likely]`
- **25 ranks total.** Each rank is +1% to base Damage/Armor/Resistance and to
  all affix values, capping at +25% at rank 25. `[Likely]` (Maxroll
  Masterworking Guide)
- At rank 25 you additionally unlock the **Capstone Bonus**: a one-time
  **+50% bonus applied to one random affix** on the item (can land on the
  Tempering affix too). This bonus is separate from - and stacks with - a
  Greater Affix's own +50%. `[Likely]`
- **Rerolling the Masterwork Affix / Capstone target:** if the Capstone
  lands on a less valuable affix, you can pay Obducite + Gold (Ancestral
  items additionally require **Neathiron**) to reroll which affix it lands
  on, repeatedly, until you get the one you want. `[Likely]`
- Cost scales linearly with current rank: roughly 10 Obducite at Quality 0
  rising to about 100 Obducite at Quality 24, per Maxroll's stated formula;
  the Capstone bonus itself additionally costs Obducite x250. Other
  materials consumed along the way: Gold, Abstruse Sigils, Forgotten Souls.
  `[Likely]`
- Primary Obducite sources: Nightmare Dungeons (especially a sigil rolled
  with the Treasure Breach affix), the Undercity via Tribute of Refinement,
  Infernal Hordes, Horadric Strongroom chests, and Mercenary bartering for a
  Masterworking Cache. `[Likely]`

## 4. Greater Affixes

- A Greater Affix guarantees the **maximum possible roll** of that affix and
  applies a further **1.5x multiplier** (commonly described elsewhere as a
  flat "+50%") on top of that max roll. `[Likely]` (Fextralife wiki, Icy
  Veins, Maxroll all agree on the 1.5x/50% figure)
- Only found on **Ancestral** Legendary and Unique items (Torment
  difficulty+). An Ancestral item is guaranteed **at least one** Greater
  Affix. `[Likely]` (Icy Veins Season 14 Occultist guide: "An Ancestral item
  will have at least one Greater Affix.")
- Marker: a distinct **star/asterisk icon** next to the affected affix line,
  plus a unique pickup sound and world/inventory icon when the item drops.
  `[Likely]`
- Maximum count per item: **up to 3** on an Ancestral Legendary (matching its
  3 natural affix slots), **up to 4** on a Unique. `[Likely]` (Fextralife
  wiki; consistent with thegamer.com's "Legendary gear can have up to three
  Greater Affixes... Uniques can have up to four").
- **Rerolling/enchanting does NOT preserve Greater status by default.** If
  you enchant (reroll) an affix that is currently a Greater Affix into a
  *different* stat, the Greater Affix is lost. `[Likely]` (thegamer.com: "If
  you reroll a Greater Affix into a different stat via enchanting, the
  Greater Affix will be lost.")
- **You cannot manufacture a new Greater Affix via enchanting** - Enchanting
  can only reroll into a normal-roll affix; Greater Affixes can only be
  found on drops or gambled from the Purveyor of Curiosities. `[Certain]`-
  adjacent, stated identically by two independent Season-14-dated Icy Veins
  guides (Tempering guide and Occultist guide): "It is important to know
  that you cannot enchant an affix into a greater affix; they can only be
  discovered."
- **Preservation trick (community-documented, not an official mechanic):**
  at the Occultist, choose to enchant/reroll the specific Greater Affix you
  want to keep and select "No Change." The affix's Greater status and value
  are kept, but the slot becomes permanently locked to only ever reroll
  that same affix going forward - this effectively protects it from being
  touched by any subsequent Horadric Cube affix-removal or reroll
  operation on that item. `[Likely]`, corroborated by two independent
  secondary write-ups (aoeah.com Season 13 guide, d4gold.com Lord of Hatred
  guide) describing the same trick with matching mechanics.
- Tempering **never touches existing Greater Affixes** - it only adds a new,
  separate Temper affix. `[Likely]` (aoeah.com: "Tempering never affects
  existing Greater Affixes").

## 5. Tempering

- Adds **exactly one** deterministic Tempering affix to Rare, Legendary,
  Unique, and Mythic Unique items via a Blacksmith recipe learned from a
  Tempering Manual. `[Likely]`, confirmed by three independent
  Season-14-dated sources (Maxroll Tempering Guide, Maxroll Blacksmith
  Upgrade & Salvage guide "Updated for Season 14," Icy Veins Tempering Guide
  for Season 14): "Rare, Legendary, Unique, and Mythic Unique items can have
  1 Tempering Affix added to them."
- Manuals are learned permanently into the Codex of Power and can then be
  reused indefinitely for a Gold + material cost; the manual's rarity
  (Magic/Rare/Legendary) gates how many candidate affixes (3-5) it offers.
  `[Likely]`
- **"Tempers: X/Y" on the tooltip is the number of REROLL ATTEMPTS
  used/available, not the number of Tempering affixes on the item.** There
  is still only ever 1 Tempering affix; Y is the attempt budget before that
  one affix locks in. This resolves the dispute directly. `[Likely]`,
  explicitly stated by the Icy Veins Season 14 Tempering guide:
  - Legendary/Ancestral Legendary items get **3 base Temper rolls**.
  - **+1 additional Temper roll per Greater Affix already on the item**
    (so up to 7 total on a 4-Greater-Affix Unique, matching Maxroll's "up
    to 7 if the item has four Greater Affixes").
  - "Once you reach the maximum attempts, the affix on the item will be
    locked in."
  - A **Scroll of Restoration** (from Dark Citadel / Infernal Hordes) fully
    restores 1 Temper roll and can be used without limit.
- Rerolling the Temper affix has a small chance to upgrade it into a
  Greater Affix on Ancestral items. `[Likely]`
- Materials scale with item rarity: Rare items use Common Salvage + Veiled
  Crystal; Legendary/Ancestral items use Legendary Salvage + Forgotten Soul
  (approximate tiers per Icy Veins).
- Tooltip marks a tempered affix with a small **anvil icon**. `[Likely]`

## 6. Enchanting at the Occultist

- **Only one affix per item can be enchanted (rerolled) at a time.**
  `[Likely]`, stated identically by Icy Veins Season 14 Occultist guide
  ("one stat per item can be modified... reroll it indefinitely") and
  overgear.com's Occultist guide ("You can change only one affix per item
  at D4 Occultist, but you can reroll it multiple times").
- **The enchant binds permanently to whichever affix slot you first pick.**
  Once you enchant a given affix, all future enchants on that item can only
  reroll that same slot - you cannot later switch which affix is the
  enchantable one. `[Likely]`, consistent across Icy Veins, overgear.com,
  and community crafting guides (aoeah.com, d4gold.com) that build "lock a
  Greater Affix" techniques on exactly this permanence.
- Each enchant offers **two randomly-rolled candidate affixes plus the
  option to keep the current roll** ("No Change"). Materials (Gold + Veiled
  Crystal for Rare/Legendary) are consumed regardless of which option you
  pick, and the item is considered enchanted (and becomes account-bound)
  even if you pick "No Change." `[Likely]`
- Inherent affixes and Unique-power affixes cannot be enchanted; only the
  normal rolled affix pool is eligible. `[Likely]` (overgear.com)
- **Uniques, Mythic Uniques, and Iconic Mythic items are now enchantable at
  the Occultist as of the current patch.** This is a change from Season 13,
  where Mythic/Unique affixes could not be touched at all (only Chaotic/
  Focused Reroll via the Horadric Cube). `[Certain]` - quoted verbatim from
  Blizzard's official, continuously-updated Diablo IV Patch Notes page
  (news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24287406/diablo-iv-patch-notes), current
  as of 2026-07-28: "Unique, Mythic Unique, and Iconic Mythic Items can now
  have affixes altered via Enchanting." The same patch-notes entry also
  states: "Added affixes from Enchanting, Transfiguration, and Tempering
  are always max rolls when added to a Mythic Unique," and "Chaotic and
  Focused Reroll have been removed for Unique and Mythic Unique items" (the
  old reroll path was retired in favor of Enchanting/Transfiguration/
  Tempering all being available now).

## 7. Horadric Cube protection of Enchanted/Tempered affixes

- **Enchanted affixes and Tempered affixes are explicitly excluded from the
  Horadric Cube's "Replace existing affix with Transfiguration Affix"
  recipe** - the Cube cannot overwrite them with that recipe.  `[Likely]`,
  quoted directly from Maxroll's Horadric Cube reference page: "Replace
  existing affix with Transfiguration Affix... (can not happen for
  Enchanted Affixes, Tempers, and Mythic Unique items)."
- This is a narrower, developer-built protection than the community "lock a
  Greater Affix via enchant + No Change" trick in section 4, which
  additionally blocks Cube affix-removal and Focus Reroll operations from
  ever landing on that slot (because the slot is no longer eligible for
  Occultist rerolling once enchanted, and separately Transfiguration
  cannot touch Tempers/Enchants per the rule above).
- Standard Transfiguration (not the "replace affix" variant) has roughly a
  98% chance of making the whole item permanently unmodifiable afterward -
  so the community-standard order of operations is: socket, temper to
  Greater Affix, Masterwork to rank 25, enchant/lock, and only then
  Transfigure, since Transfiguration should be the last step performed.
  `[Likely]` (d4gold.com)

## 8. Sockets, gems, and Runewords

- Sockets are added at the **Jeweler** using a **Scattered Prism** (drops
  from World Bosses, Treasure Goblins, Legion events), and only on
  Ancestral-quality gear. Two-handed weapons can hold 2 sockets. `[Likely]`
- **Gems and Runes share the same sockets.** Socketing a Rune into a gemmed
  item removes the gems (and vice versa) - you choose per item whether you
  want the gem bonus or a Runeword. `[Likely]` (nexttier.pro Season 14 gems
  guide)
- Gem tiers, low to high: base tiers craftable at the Jeweler up through
  **Grand** (needs Gem Fragments + Forgotten Souls), then two Cube-only
  tiers above that:
  - **Horadric** gem: 5 Grand gems of the same type (Amalgamation), roughly
    a 28% weapon multiplier.
  - **Flawless Horadric** gem: 5 Horadric gems (= 25 Grand gems total),
    roughly a 32% weapon multiplier.
  `[Likely]` (nexttier.pro). A Flawless Horadric Skull specifically was
  separately reported (iggm.com) as requiring approximately 25 million Skull
  Fragments plus large Gold and Forgotten Soul costs end-to-end - this is
  presented by that source as an extreme, "luxury asset" grind, not typical
  gem crafting.
- **Runewords**: introduced in the Lord of Hatred expansion, built from two
  runes per socket pair - a **Rune of Ritual** (generates "Offering" on a
  trigger condition) and a **Rune of Invocation** (spends Offering to evoke
  an empowered version of a class skill, some with an "Overflow" bonus
  effect at high Offering). Every class shares two skills and one aspect in
  the Runeword system. `[Likely]` (Icy Veins Runewords guide, page labeled
  Season 14, though see Gaps re: a possibly-stale Mythic-crafting-via-runes
  section on that same page).

## 9. "Mythics 3.0"

- **Every Unique item can now roll or be upgraded into a Mythic version.**
  A Mythic version of a Unique has two defining traits: its Unique Power
  effect is **boosted by roughly 30% and forced to its cap**, and **every
  other affix on the item is forced to its maximum roll**. `[Likely]`,
  corroborated independently by an iggm.com PTR-preview writeup ("the
  unique effect is boosted by 30% and also reaches its cap... every
  attribute on the item will be set to the maximum possible value") and a
  game8 Season 14 guide ("all Unique items can now be dropped as Mythic
  Uniques which are always tagged as Ancestral and have increased Unique
  Powers").
  - "Legacy" Mythic items (the old, pre-3.0 Mythic Uniques with no normal
    Unique counterpart, e.g. Andariel's Visage, Doombringer, The
    Grandfather) were folded into the same system and heavily rebalanced:
    they now roll affixes randomly like any other Mythic, all max-rolled,
    though most retain one fixed affix that can no longer be changed.
    `[Likely]` (iggm.com)
- **Horadric Cube upgrade path:** a Unique item (Item Power 850+ per
  game8) can be converted into a random Mythic version of the same item
  category by consuming **Pandemonium Fragments** (a Season 14 seasonal
  material). The exact Fragment cost was reduced mid-season: 5 Fragments
  at Season 14 launch, **reduced to 4 Fragments** in a hotfix, per the
  official Blizzard patch notes page ("Reduced the cost of the Upgrade to
  Mythic recipe on the Horadric Cube from 5 to 4 Pandemonium Fragments").
  `[Certain]` for the cost reduction itself (direct Blizzard patch-note
  quote); the recipe's category-matching rules (weapons stay within their
  weapon category; Rings/Amulets share one pool; armor draws from any of
  the 5 armor slots) are `[Likely]`, sourced to iggm.com's PTR writeup.
  The Blizzard patch notes page also confirms this recipe was renamed from
  "Upgrade to Mythic" to "Craft" for naming consistency, and that "The
  Upgrade to Mythic recipe in the Horadric Cube now always creates an item
  for the same gear slot" and "Mythic Unique Items cannot be used in the
  Upgrade to Mythic Unique recipe" (you cannot feed an existing Mythic back
  into the recipe). `[Certain]`
- **The "equip only one crafted Mythic Unique" limit has been REMOVED.**
  This is now settled, not disputed: `[Certain]`, a verbatim Blizzard blue
  post quoted by mobalytics.gg dated to the Patch 3.1.1a announcement
  (released July 16, 2026): *"We've heard your feedback on Season of Death
  Awakening, including Mythic unique drop rates, equip limits, and more. In
  a patch releasing today, we're increasing the drop rate for Mythics from
  the Corrupted Reaper boss fight and removing equip limits for Mythic
  Uniques."* Prior to this hotfix, items crafted via the Horadric Cube were
  flagged "Crafted," and only one Crafted Mythic Unique could be equipped
  at a time (naturally-dropped Mythics had no such restriction) - this was
  a major community complaint through the first ~2.5 weeks of the season,
  documented on the official Blizzard forums, before Blizzard disabled the
  limit server-side in 3.1.1a. `[Likely]` for the pre-hotfix mechanics
  description (iggm.com, us.forums.blizzard.com thread), `[Certain]` for
  the removal itself.
- Blizzard has separately signaled a bigger change coming next season: in
  Season 15, the Horadric Cube's Mythic recipe is expected to become a
  **direct, deterministic upgrade** (place a specific Unique, get that
  same item's Mythic version) rather than the current random-Mythic-from-
  category output. `[Unverified]` - reported by iggm.com as a developer
  statement, but not yet cross-confirmed against an official Blizzard
  source, and Season 15 has not shipped as of this snapshot.

## Gaps

- **Exact current Ancestral / Mythic Item Power ceiling is not fully
  settled.** One Season-14-dated game8 page states Ancestral Legendaries
  are 900 Item Power and Mythic Uniques (the old "Uber Unique" pool) are
  925 Item Power; a different game8 page states Mythic Uniques "always
  drop at 800 item Power"; a 2024 (pre-Lord-of-Hatred) TheGamer article
  says Ancestral was 800 at that time. These cannot all be current
  simultaneously - the 800 figures are most likely stale holdovers from
  before the Lord of Hatred level-cap/item-power increase. Re-verify the
  exact number in-game or against a single dated source before writing it
  into D4Companion.
- **Runewords Mythic-crafting recipe may be stale on the page cited.** The
  Icy Veins "Season 14"-labeled Runewords guide describes crafting a
  *specific* named Mythic Unique from 1 Resplendent Spark + a fixed
  3-rune combo (e.g. Ring of Starless Skies = Spark + Ohm + Tzic + Cem).
  But two Season 14 sources for Mythics 3.0 (game8, iggm.com) state that,
  as of Season 14, the Jeweler/Blacksmith Mythic recipes now produce a
  *random* Mythic from a category pool and that "you can no longer craft
  specific Mythic Uniques at the Jeweler." These directly conflict; the
  Icy Veins Runewords page was likely not fully updated for the Mythics
  3.0 overhaul even though its header says "Season 14." Do not trust the
  specific-recipe rune combinations without re-checking in-client.
  - Also unresolved: whether the Jeweler/Blacksmith Mythic crafting still
    consumes Runes at all post-3.0, versus purely Resplendent Sparks + Gold
    as one source suggested for the Blacksmith path.
- **Whether the enchant-Unique/Mythic/Iconic-Mythic change and the
  guaranteed-2nd-affix-on-Unique-drop change belong to patch 3.1.2
  specifically, or an earlier point in the season, is not pinned down.**
  The official cumulative Blizzard patch notes page confirms both are part
  of the *current* live state as of 2026-07-28, but Maxroll's dedicated
  3.1.2-only writeup lists just 3 unrelated bug fixes, suggesting these
  enchant/affix changes actually landed in an earlier 3.1.x patch that
  simply appears further down the same cumulative page. This does not
  affect whether the mechanic is currently true, only which patch number
  introduced it.
- **Whether Rare items (not just Legendary/Unique) can be enchanted was
  not directly confirmed for the current patch** - sources describe
  enchanting "Rare or Legendary" gear but the emphasis throughout is
  Legendary/Ancestral; not separately stress-tested here.
- **The Season 15 "direct Mythic upgrade" claim is a single secondary
  source (iggm.com) reporting an unconfirmed developer statement.** Not
  verified against an official Blizzard post, and Season 15 has not
  shipped.
- Did not find a definitive, official (Blizzard) statement of the maximum
  number of Greater Affixes obtainable via Masterworking's Capstone bonus
  interacting with an item that already has the maximum natural count
  (4 on a Unique) - the interaction is described consistently by secondary
  sources but not by a primary one.
